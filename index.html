V6 browser version: alarms work while this page remains open. It can open websites and search the web. Say “Bro” once to start a conversation session. Then you can give commands without repeating “Bro”. Say “stop listening”, “bye bro”, or “goodbye” to end the session.

Android system alarms, phone calls, contacts, and direct app/device controls require a native Android app.
